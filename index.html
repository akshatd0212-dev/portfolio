Roots & Trajectory

My Journey & Background

From school days in Dhanbad to coding during a global pandemic, up to pursuing Computer Science in Jaipur.

The Origin: Dhanbad & DPS Hirak

I was born and brought up in Dhanbad, Jharkhand, known as India's coal capital. I completed my schooling at Delhi Public School (DPS) Hirak Branch, where my fascination with logic, mathematics, and how devices operate under the hood first took root.

Rather than just using software, I was always curious about the machinery behind it — dissecting how components interact, why computers run fast or throttle, and how lines of text translate into digital experiences.

📍 Dhanbad Native 🏫 DPS Hirak Alum ⚙️ System Tinkerer

Hardware Fascination & Tech Hobbies

Beyond coding on the screen, my biggest passion lies in computer hardware and architecture. I love assembling custom PC builds, tuning thermal dynamics, optimizing BIOS/UEFI configurations, and troubleshooting tricky motherboard and peripheral issues.

Understanding computing from the physical silicon level up gives me an instinct for writing efficient, lightweight, and performant code that respects system resources.

🖥️ Custom PC Assembly 🌡️ Thermal Benchmarking 🔧 Hardware Diagnostics 🔌 IoT & Electronics
